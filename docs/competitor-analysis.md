## 2. Adopted Improvements for Rao's Furniture Paradise (Within WoodenStreet Architecture)

1. **Dual Enquiry-First Conversion Pattern (from Damro + RK Smart + WoodenStreet):**  
   Since Phase 1 has no payment gateway or verified pricing for all items, every product card and PDP cleanly supports **Enquiry Basket ("Add to Enquiry")**, **Request Price**, and **Showroom Consultation**, mirroring WoodenStreet's cart/wishlist ergonomics without fake discounts.
2. **Honest Pricing & Zero-Gimmick Policy (inspired by Bontrue):**  
   Display clear nstration prices where provided or clean **"Price on Request"** labels, strictly avoiding WoodenStreet's countdown timers, fake MRP strikethroughs, or unverified sale banners.
3. **Interactive "Shop the Look" Room Hotspots (inspired by IKEA + WoodenStreet lifestyle sections):**  
   Let Visakhapatnam buyers inspect complete room scenes (Modern Living Room, Elegant Bedroom, Contemporary Dining Room) with clickable hotspots revealing dimensions, materials, and quick enquiry actions.
4. **Structured Customization Consultation Builder (inspired by BoConcept + WoodenStreet Custom Furniture):**  
   Allow visitors to specify Furniture Type, Material, Color, Dimensions, Seating Capacity, and Approximate Budget, generating a structured consultation summary ready to copy or share once verified contact channels are active.
5. **Daba Gardens Local Showroom Anchor (inspired by Damro + WoodenStreet city pages):**  
   Surface the exact Saraswati Park / Daba Gardens landmark, embedded map/directions CTA, and physical inspection benefits right within the storefront flow, tailored to Visakhapatnam homeowners and offices.

## 3. Excluded Elements (Intentional Guardrails)

- No countdown urgency timers or synthetic "Up to 75% Off" banners.
- No fake ratings (`4.8 ★ (2,410)`), stock scarcity badges (`Only 2 left`), or unverified customer counts.
- No unrelated contracting/plumbing marketplace clutter (avoiding Livincart's service sprawl).
- No Scandinavian minimalism that strips away WoodenStreet's rich category grid and room-by-room carousel density.
